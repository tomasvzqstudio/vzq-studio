Services

We help you get your film made

Getting a short film made in Spain takes as much paperwork as filmmaking. We have been through it several times, so now we help others do it: from procedures and financing to the pitch deck that wins over a jury.

Executive production Design and creative consulting
01

Executive production consulting

We guide you through the administrative, legal and financial side of your project so you can focus on the film. We do it the same way as on our own productions: organised, with clear deadlines and a realistic financing plan.

ICAA procedures

  • Registering your short film with the ICAA
  • Notifying the start-of-shoot date
  • Spanish nationality certificate
  • Age rating

Financing and contracts

  • Budget and financing plan
  • Applying for and justifying regional grants
  • Co-productions between companies and regions
  • Co-production, director and composer contracts

And also

  • Guidance on setting up your own production company
  • Distribution and festival strategy
  • Production schedule and plan

02

Pitch deck design and creative consulting

A good project badly presented loses to a weaker one told well. We design and structure your pitch deck for funding calls, labs, markets or crowdfunding, and help you define the film's visual identity. Our decks have already secured grants from the Junta de Castilla y León and the Government of Cantabria.

Some of the pitch decks we have designed: Camila, Clemencia, El Ciervo, Lo que está en juego and La historia de Ma Salimata. Click any of them to see it larger.

Pitch decks

  • Structure tailored to each call
  • Review of synopsis, statements and texts
  • Design and layout
  • Versions adapted to each grant or lab

Graphic design

  • Visual identity for the film
  • Posters and social media assets
  • Press kit and festival materials

Creative consulting

  • Visual approach and references
  • Art direction and costume
  • Moodboards for funders and crew
