Lo que está en juego

A short film by Tomás Vázquez, written by Rubén Jiménez

After losing an online sports bet while having dinner with his girlfriend's parents, Christian must resort to desperate measures to pay the bill.

Director Tomás Vázquez
Screenplay Rubén Jiménez
Genre Black comedy
Cast Álex Fernández
Shooting Madrid, 2027
Production V·Z·Q Studio, La Mala Fama, Estudio Clementine

Synopsis

In a small sports centre in Australia, the young Japanese tennis player Sakura Watanabe faces a game point under her opponent's astonished gaze. The tension is at its peak as she prepares to serve.

On the other side of the world, in Madrid, Christian (28) is facing his own match point: he is about to meet the parents of his girlfriend, Ángela (28). Determined to make a good impression, his mission is to pay the bill at the restaurant he has taken them to.

What the family doesn't know is that Christian is hiding his phone under the table, following a sports bet on which all his money depends.

When the bet collapses and he finds he has thirty cents left in his account, Christian spirals into a desperate race to get the money before the bill arrives. Each attempt is wilder than the last: lies to his mother, blackmailing an ex and decisions that strip away his dignity in front of the people he most needs to impress.

The project

Lo que está en juego tackles one of the most urgent and least discussed problems of our generation: the normalisation of sports betting and its devastating consequences on young people's lives.

Inspired by personal experience and first-hand accounts, the short film sets out to portray, with a naturalistic and twistedly comic approach, how what starts as a harmless game can become a bottomless pit.

The project is conceived as a strongly personal piece with a clear social and cultural commitment, able to spark debate on a highly relevant issue in contemporary Spain.

Current status

The short film is a co-production between V·Z·Q Studio, La Mala Fama and Estudio Clementine. Shooting is planned in Madrid in 2027, with the lead cast and most locations already confirmed.

The short will also serve as the first piece in the narrative universe of the future feature film of the same name.
